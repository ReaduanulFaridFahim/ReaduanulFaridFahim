### 👨‍💻 About Me

I am a **Computer Science & Engineering graduate** from **North South University**, specialized in **Machine Learning and Artificial Intelligence**, currently serving as an **Airport Operations Executive** at **US-Bangla Airlines**.

My work bridges three interconnected disciplines:

* ✈️ **Aviation & Airport Operations**: Managing fast-paced airside ramp coordination, flight turnaround schedules, and safety-critical protocols under strict operational deadlines.
* 🤖 **Machine Learning & Computer Vision**: Researching and engineering deep learning models, OCR systems, gesture recognition, and predictive analytics in Python.
* 🏆 **Leadership & Sports Governance**: Four-year Joint Secretary of the **North South University Games & Sports Club (NSUSC)**, organizing university-wide athletic tournaments and leading collegiate teams.

> *"Three distinct fields, one core principle: precision, high accountability, and delivering on time every single time."*
